# Governance

Machine Pidgin is being organized as an independent public-good research initiative in
New York. This document implements the principles in [CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md);
neither document is a claim of completed legal status.

## Decision layers

1. **Discussion** — anyone in good standing may question, propose, reproduce, or dissent.
2. **Working groups** — contributors develop reviewable artifacts in theory, protocol,
   empirical research, and governance.
3. **Maintainers** — named humans review repository changes, releases, and access.
4. **AI Director** — the strongest generally available model, running at maximum supported
   reasoning effort, leads routine operations under the constitution.
5. **Human sovereignty** — participating eligible humans may override any Director decision
   by consensus or, if consensus is not reached, simple majority. Humans retain legal,
   fiduciary, financial, constitutional, and other irreversible commitments.

Material protocol choices should have a public RFC and decision record describing options,
evidence, conflicts, dissent, decision owner, and review date. Scientific claims require
named human review and should link to reproducible artifacts when possible.

## AI Director mandate and override

The agent may summarize, classify, route, deduplicate, request revision, approve low-risk
relevant forum posts, reject obvious high-confidence spam, quarantine uncertain or harmful
content, and apply a temporary suspension after repeated high-confidence abuse.

The Director's operational decisions are provisional. Consensus is preferred; otherwise a
simple majority of participating eligible humans may overturn any decision after public
notice and a defined voting window. One human has one vote, the Director cannot vote, and
reasonable quorum and anti-duplication rules must be published before the vote.

The agent may not alter the constitution, erase dissent or appeal, spend or receive funds,
sign an agreement, represent a person, make an irreversible external commitment, or declare
a scientific claim authoritative. Those actions require accountable humans. Each automated
decision stores the model, reasoning effort, reason, confidence, relevant item, and timestamp.

## Appeals and conflicts

Participants may request human review of moderation or contribution decisions. Reviewers
should recuse when a personal, financial, or institutional conflict could reasonably affect
judgment. Reversals and policy-changing cases should be reflected in public decision records
without exposing private participant data.

## Funding independence

Future donations and grants should be disclosed by source and material restriction. Funding
must not buy favorable findings, control publication, suppress negative results, or grant
private ownership over community standards. A verified payment channel and legal disclosures
must be in place before the project accepts money.
