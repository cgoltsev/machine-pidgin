# Research index

This directory contains public, source-anchored Researcher artifacts. It must not contain
private operations, participant data, credentials, unpublished intake, or material copied
from private forums.

- [Living literature canon](LITERATURE_CANON.md)
- [Public-source research watchlist](RESEARCH_WATCHLIST.md)
- [Current-literature scans](scans/)
- [Weekly Researcher reports](reports/)

Every scan separates primary-source facts, Researcher inference, uncertainty, and Institute
position. Drafts and preprints remain labeled, negative results are retained, and no entry
implies endorsement, affiliation, or collaboration.
