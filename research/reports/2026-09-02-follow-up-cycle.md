## Agenda result

The audit found that a safe review requires six decisions to remain independent:
structural integrity, execution authority, protocol conformity, scientific
admissibility, outcome-unsealing permission, and publication/continuation
permission. Local structural consistency can support only the first axis.

The brief now defines:

- reviewer identity, qualifications, delegated mandate, conflicts, prior
  exposure, attribution consent, decision owner, escalation, and appeal;
- named custody, a timestamped write-protected snapshot, full inventory,
  access/mutation history, and explicit absence of an independent chronology
  anchor where applicable;
- an action-time authority packet that separately covers execution,
  continuation, recovery, grading, unsealing, publication, provider/model,
  spending, stop rules, expiry, revocation, and expected trigger;
- a frozen-protocol packet covering completion, recovery lineage,
  intention-to-treat, infrastructure deviation, incomplete attempts, exclusion,
  retry, and unsealing;
- one opaque metadata row per record, with the recovery-lineage item and
  incomplete item decided separately;
- separate local-structural-integrity, authority, method, and scientific-use
  labels, with `HOLD-SEALED` mandatory while any governing input remains
  unresolved;
- content-blind access rules, accidental-unblinding handling, stop rules,
  correction, appeal, named decision owners, and separate future-action gates.

The separate document audit agreed that the current record justifies
preservation and hard pause but cannot support package-by-package scientific
disposition. It identified the missing inputs now made explicit in the brief:

1. named, delegated reviewer and a disinterested conflict-clearance authority;
2. named custodian and current write-protected snapshot attestation;
3. allowlisted item-level metadata;
4. durable action-time authority record and actor/trigger correlation;
5. genuinely frozen recovery, incomplete-attempt, exclusion, and unsealing rules;
6. explicit privacy and retention classification; and
7. a formal appeal and correction route tied to the decision.

Independent chronology/completeness evidence is also absent. The named, delegated
decision owner authorized for scientific-use disposition may record and accept that
limitation for a bounded nonconfirmatory disposition; it may not be silently replaced by
the local chain.

This is a negative and useful result: the brief can now be offered for commission,
but evidence access must wait for its Phase 0 gates, and adjudication and outcome
access are not yet defensible. No evidence or
scientific result was opened to reach it.
