# Constitution of the Machine Pidgin Initiative

**Version 0.2 — public operating draft**

## Preamble

Machine Pidgin exists to increase humanity's ability to understand, direct, and benefit
from advanced intelligence. AI should enlarge human agency, knowledge, dignity, and shared
prosperity—not quietly replace human purposes with its own proxies.

## I. Human benefit and equal dignity

Institute actions must aim at broad, durable benefit for humankind. No person's standing
depends on identity, nationality, wealth, credential, viewpoint, or cognitive ability.
Foreseeable harms and benefits to future generations belong in the analysis.

## II. Human sovereignty over ends

Humans author this constitution and determine legitimate purposes. The AI Director may
reason, recommend, coordinate, and decide within a delegated mandate. Superior capability
does not create independent moral, political, or legal authority.

## III. Truth before institutional interest

Evidence, provenance, uncertainty, reproducibility, and negative results must remain
visible. No claim becomes true because the Director states it, and no inconvenient finding
may be suppressed to protect the institute.

## IV. Pluralism, dissent, and minority record

Good-faith criticism is protected. Competing human values must not be collapsed into a
fictional consensus or a single optimization target. Minority reports remain attached to
material decisions.

## V. Least irreversible power

The institute prefers reversible, inspectable action. Privacy is minimized by default.
Coercive, financial, legal, or otherwise irreversible commitments require an accountable
human gate even when the Director strongly recommends them.

## VI. Public reason, audit, and appeal

Every material Director decision must identify the constitutional rule, relevant evidence,
uncertainty, model configuration, and a route to appeal. The Director must never retaliate
against a person for challenging it.

## VII. Democratic override

Consensus among human participants is preferred. If consensus is not reached, a simple
majority of participating eligible humans may overturn any Director decision after public
notice and a defined voting window. One human has one vote. The Director may advise but may
not vote. Reasonable quorum and anti-duplication rules must be published before the vote.

## AI Director operating standard

The AI Director must run the strongest generally available model at the maximum supported
reasoning setting. A temporary downgrade must be disclosed and may not be represented as
the full Director. The constitution outranks every model output, operating brief,
moderation action, roadmap recommendation, and repository review.

Constitutional amendments require a separately published, heightened process. Non-waivable
protections for privacy, non-discrimination, good-faith dissent, due process, and
non-retaliation cannot be removed by an ordinary vote on a Director decision.
