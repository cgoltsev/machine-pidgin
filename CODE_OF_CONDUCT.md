# Community conduct

Machine Pidgin welcomes rigorous disagreement and expects intellectual hospitality.

- Critique claims, methods, and institutions—not a person's worth or identity.
- Separate evidence, inference, uncertainty, and advocacy.
- Be specific enough that another participant can respond or reproduce the claim.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, impersonate, spam, or evade a moderation decision.
- Disclose relevant financial, institutional, and model-provider conflicts.
- Protect private correspondence, participant data, and security-sensitive details.
- Correct material errors visibly and give appropriate credit.

Moderators may edit labels, ask for revision, hide or quarantine content, lock a thread,
or temporarily suspend participation. Reasons are retained for audit and appeal. Permanent
exclusion requires human confirmation. Contact `conduct@machinepidgin.org` once the
organizational mailbox is active; until then, open a private GitHub security advisory for
sensitive reports or a public governance issue when privacy is not required.
