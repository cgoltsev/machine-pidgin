# Public research roadmap

## Replicate

- reproduce Benchmark 001 independently;
- preregister fresh held-out natural tasks and analysis;
- add cross-vendor model families and repeated stochastic samples;
- report authoring time, repair cost, token cost, and semantic usefulness alongside exact output adherence.

## Isolate the intervention

- separate field structure, interpretation prompt, examples, final checking, and output schema in a factorial study;
- compare SPEAR with concise checklists, JSON Schema, formal methods, and ordinary prose with equivalent attention cues;
- test whether authority, precedence, vocabulary, stop, and check fields each earn their authoring cost.

## Increase consequence and realism

- long-horizon tool-use tasks with spending, publication, deletion, credential, and access boundaries;
- multi-party specifications with conflicting values, minority reports, and appeal;
- distribution shift, infeasible constraints, adversarial paraphrase, and specification gaming;
- domain studies led by experts who did not design SPEAR.

## Standardize only after evidence

- versioned conformance corpus and reference parser;
- accessible authoring tools and repair messages;
- public RFCs, decision records, compatibility policy, and independent maintainers;
- a published null-result threshold at which the protocol claim narrows or is abandoned.
